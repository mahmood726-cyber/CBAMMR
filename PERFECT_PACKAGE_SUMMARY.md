## 🚀 What's New in v8.2.0

### Major Feature Additions:

#### 1. **Professional Visualization Suite** (10 plot methods)
All advanced methods now have publication-ready plots:
- `plot.cbamm_permutation_test()` - Null distribution visualization
- `plot.cbamm_bootstrap_ci()` - Bootstrap distribution with CI
- `plot.cbamm_quantile_ma()` - Quantile forest plot
- `plot.cbamm_threshold_analysis()` - Robustness curve
- `plot.cbamm_evpi()` - EVPI breakdown
- `plot.cbamm_decision_curve()` - Net benefit curves
- `plot.cbamm_prob_best()` - Rankogram & SUCRA
- `plot.cbamm_nnt_meta()` - NNT visualization
- `plot.cbamm_individualized_effect()` - Patient-specific effects
- `plot.cbamm_rmst_meta()` - RMST survival

**Features:**
- Professional ggplot2 aesthetics
- Automatic annotations and labels
- Clear interpretative subtitles
- Publication-ready quality
- Customizable with standard ggplot2 syntax

#### 2. **Bayesian Methods Suite** (2 functions)
Principled Bayesian alternatives:
- `cbamm_bayesian_bootstrap()` - Full posterior with Dirichlet weights
- `cbamm_bayesian_metareg()` - MCMC meta-regression

**Advantages:**
- Full posterior distributions
- Prior incorporation capability
- Natural uncertainty quantification
- Probability statements
- Decision-theoretic approaches

#### 3. **Comprehensive Sensitivity Analysis** (2 functions)
Robustness assessment tools:
- `cbamm_sensitivity_analysis()` - Multi-method sensitivity
  - Leave-one-out analysis
  - Small study exclusion
  - High variance exclusion
  - **Robustness score (0-100)** with interpretation
- `cbamm_publication_bias_sensitivity()` - Publication bias tests
  - Trim-and-fill analysis
  - Egger's test
  - Combined recommendations

**Unique Feature:** Automated robustness scoring
- 90-100: ★★★★★ Very robust
- 75-89: ★★★★☆ Robust
- 60-74: ★★★☆☆ Moderately robust
- <60: ★★☆☆☆ Fragile

#### 4. **Real-World Documentation** (2 vignettes)
Comprehensive learning resources:
- `vignettes/distribution-free-methods.Rmd` (~600 lines)
  - Case study: SSRIs in elderly depression
  - 4 methods demonstrated
  - Complete workflow
  - Clinical interpretations

- `vignettes/clinical-decision-tools.Rmd` (~700 lines)
  - Case study: NOACs vs warfarin
  - 5 decision tools demonstrated
  - Integrated framework
  - Policy recommendations

#### 5. **Complete Test Suite** (105+ tests)
Quality assurance:
