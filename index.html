Bayesian Normal-Normal Meta-Analysis

A fully Bayesian random-effects meta-analysis by grid approximation. Heterogeneity SD τ gets a half-Cauchy(0, s) prior and the overall mean μ a flat prior; the app returns the marginal posterior of μ with its 95% credible interval, plus a Bayesian prediction interval for a future study. Fully offline — no network, no dependencies.

Input

Variance is the squared standard error of the estimate (vi = SE²). Use the analysis scale; for ratios enter log RR/OR/HR and toggle log below.
Larger s = more diffuse prior on between-study heterogeneity (default 0.5). A wide s makes the posterior mean of μ approach the frequentist REML estimate.
Provenance. Model yi ~ N(theta_i, vi), theta_i ~ N(mu, tau²); prior tau ~ half-Cauchy(0, s), mu flat. The marginal posterior of μ is a mixture over the τ grid of N(muHat(tau), V(tau)) weighted by p(tau|y); intervals are density quantiles, not mean ± z·SE. Grid approximation is used because the target is 1–2 parameters and unimodal.
